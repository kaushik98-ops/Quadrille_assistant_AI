Employee handbook. Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:30 Monday to Friday. Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused leave of up to 5 days can be carried over into the next year.

Remote work: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with receipts. The laptop refresh cycle is every 3 years.
