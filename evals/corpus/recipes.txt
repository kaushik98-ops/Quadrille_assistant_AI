Sourdough bread recipe. Mix 500 g flour, 350 g water, 100 g active starter and 10 g salt. Let the dough rise for 4 to 5 hours at room temperature, folding every hour. Bake at 230 C for 20 minutes with steam, then 20 minutes more without steam.

Tomato soup: Roast 1 kg tomatoes with garlic and onion for 40 minutes at 200 C. Blend with 500 ml vegetable stock and simmer for 15 minutes. Serve with basil.
