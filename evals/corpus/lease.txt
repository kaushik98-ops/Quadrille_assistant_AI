RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT. This lease is between Maple Court Apartments (the landlord) and the tenant for unit 4B. The lease term is 12 months starting on 1 March. Monthly rent is due on the first day of each month. A late fee of 50 dollars applies after the fifth day.
=== PAGE ===
Security deposit: The tenant pays a security deposit equal to one month of rent. The deposit is returned within 30 days after move-out, minus documented damage. Early termination requires 60 days written notice to the landlord and a fee equal to one month of rent. Pets are not allowed without written permission.
